TECHFEST  /  FLIGHT NOTES

Mission brief.

Techfest is IIT Bombay’s student-organised science and technology festival. Its 30th edition brings builders, researchers and curious visitors together in Powai.

The orbiter is an original digital sculpture for this edition’s independent design concept. Drag it to inspect the solar arrays and antenna, or focus it and use the arrow keys.
